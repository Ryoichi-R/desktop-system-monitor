# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Desktop System Monitor welcoming,
safe, and respectful for everyone. This applies to maintainers, contributors,
issue reporters, reviewers, and other participants, regardless of background,
identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and in good faith;
- welcome constructive feedback and differing technical viewpoints;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and observable behavior rather than people;
- respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop an interaction; and
- take responsibility for mistakes and help repair harm when possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption;
- discriminatory, insulting, sexualized, or demeaning language or imagery;
- personal attacks or unwanted personal attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern; or
- other conduct that a reasonable person would consider unsafe or
  inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone is
officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting a concern

Do not place sensitive details in a public issue. Use the repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** private form and prefix the report title
with `[Code of Conduct]`. The private form is also the designated confidential
channel for conduct reports; a personal maintainer email address is not
required. If the private form is unavailable, do not disclose the report
publicly. Use GitHub's platform reporting tools for urgent abuse and wait for a
verified private project channel to become available.

Include only the information needed to understand the incident. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Information may be shared
only when needed to investigate, protect participants, comply with law, or
provide due process. Retaliation against a reporter or witness is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code
of Conduct. They may edit or remove content, issue a correction or warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude a participant. The
response will be proportionate to the impact, severity, repetition, and
available evidence. Maintainers with a conflict of interest must not decide the
matter alone.
