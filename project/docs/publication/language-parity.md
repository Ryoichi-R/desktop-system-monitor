# Japanese/English parity checklist

The detailed Japanese content in `README.md` is the pre-release source of truth.
Its bilingual landing section and `README.en.md` must describe the same release
status. Before each public release, compare them for product version, release
channel, supported architectures, download status, privacy behavior, and known
limitations. English may be shorter but must not claim broader support or
stronger accuracy.

For display-width changes, also compare the Standard (280 DIP) and Reduced
(150 DIP) names, default Standard behavior, Settings/tray entry points,
omitted Reduced details (including conditional network peak rows), scale behavior, EdgeFade outer-window limitation, and
restart persistence in both language surfaces.
